Pin Scheduler privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Pin Scheduler is a personal Android app that lets its user schedule Pins to their own Pinterest account.

What the app stores

  • Images you choose, and the titles, descriptions, links and times you enter, stored only on your device until they are posted or you delete them.
  • Your Pinterest access and refresh tokens and app credentials, stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

What the app sends

The app sends your Pins and requests for your board list directly to Pinterest's official API (api.pinterest.com) using Pinterest OAuth. It does not send your data to any other server, does not use analytics or ads, and does not sell or share data.

Deleting your data

Use Disconnect in the app's Settings to remove your Pinterest tokens, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. You can also revoke the app's access from your Pinterest account settings.
